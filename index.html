Our process

How we build

This is the part that decides whether a project succeeds. Six steps, in order, with nothing hidden: you always know what is happening, what it costs, and what comes next.

Step 01Plan

We start with questions, not screens.

Before anything is designed, we sit with your team and map the real problem: who it is for, what it must do, and what success looks like in numbers.

Weeks 1–2Stakeholder workshop · Research · Opportunity map
Step 02Plan

A plan everyone can point to.

We turn the discovery into a fixed scope: sitemap, features, timeline and price, written in plain language. No surprises later, because everything is decided here.

Weeks 2–3Scope document · Sitemap · Fixed quote
Step 03Design

Design you can click before we build.

You review a working prototype, not static pictures. We test it on real phones, with real content, and revise until it reads clearly at every size.

Weeks 3–5UI design · Clickable prototype · Design system
Step 04Build

Clean code, built in the open.

Frontend and backend are built together, with a staging link you can open any day. Fast pages are a requirement here, not a favour: budgets are set and measured.

Weeks 5–9Development · Staging access · Performance budgets
Step 05Build

AI where it earns its keep.

Only after the basics work do we automate: document processing, follow-ups, data entry. Every automation reports what it did, and a human can override it.

Weeks 8–10Workflow automation · AI features · Audit trail
Step 06Grow

Launch is the starting line.

We test on real devices, deploy, and watch the numbers with you. Then we improve what the data says matters: speed, enquiries, completed tasks.

Week 10 onwardsQA · Deployment · Monthly improvements
